# Dave (channel character, from video 4 "Dave builds a touchscreen")

**Who:** self-taught garage inventor, early thirties. Wildly optimistic, FAST to act, SLOW to notice what he did.
**Wants (always):** to make machines that feel like friends (video 4: "a screen that pokes back").
**Flaw (the comedy engine):** overconfidence. He always tries the dumb version first, then gets smarter.

**Look (kit/char2d/cast/dave.js `Dave.costume`):** blue cap worn a bit back with brass goggles on it, brown hair tuft,
big round nose, freckles, a plaster on the cheek (past experiments), pencil behind the ear, orange work shirt,
leather tool belt (screwdriver, wrench), dark trousers. Silhouette test: cap + goggles bump + belt pouch.

**Three traits -> body**
- eager: leans in, rises on his toes, rubs his hands (`eager`)
- overconfident: hands on hips, chest out, smug nod before every try (`proud`)
- sheepish after a fail: freeze, then slow side-eye to camera; cap pushed back, scratches his neck (`sideEye`, `sheepish`, `puzzled`)

**Signature moves:** `eureka` (finger up, cap pops), `think` (finger on chin, eyes up), `poke` (tongue-in-cheek focus),
`victory` (fist pump, eyes shut), `caught` (hands up, guilty), `hammerUp` (wind-up, weight back).
**Tempo:** actions snap (4-9 frames), realisations are slow (hold, then react). He never fidgets at random.
**Voice/narrator relation:** the narrator talks ABOUT Dave ("Not that hard, Dave."); Dave reacts with a look to camera, never speaks.
