# Fun rules (after feedback on video 1, 2026-11-09)

Viewer feedback on the engine Short: "too detailed, too technical. Want more animated, more fun, learn one new thing, talk normal, not professional."
Stats after ~9 h: 4 views, 2 likes. The YouTube title was left as "Upload video" (never set): always check title + description before publishing.

## What went wrong in video 1
- 11 part names in 60 s (cylinder, piston, intake valve, compression, spark plug, connecting rod, crankshaft, exhaust valve, RPM, G, radiator).
- 6 big numbers (100/s, 10:1, 2,000 °C, 6,000 RPM, 2,000 G, 1/3). Each one is a fact to hold in your head.
- Textbook shape: "Step one... step four". No character, no problem, no joke, no surprise.
- Realistic metal product render: beautiful, but cold. Nothing to care about.
- Narrator sounded like a lecture.

## Rules for the next videos (from the skills)
1. ONE idea per video, ONE "wow, I didn't know that" at the end (ig-reel, xdis video-explainer: 1 concept, ~9 lines).
2. A character with a want. Shape: hook -> it wants something -> it goes wrong -> the twist (the science) -> payoff -> end that loops to the start (hand-drawn-cartoon, 3d-toy-character briefs).
3. At most 3 new words per video. Name a thing only if the joke or the payoff needs it.
4. At most 1-2 numbers, shown as pictures (a whale, a bathtub), never a list.
5. Talk like a friend: contractions, short lines, "okay so...", reactions ("wait, what?"). Run it through ig-human before recording.
6. Acting beats talking: show it, then say less. Every action: anticipation -> move -> squash -> settle. Characters never frozen (blink, breathe). Hold 0.3-0.75 s before the payoff.
7. Something new every 1-2 s; one read at a time (reads sheet); first frame is a finished picture, not black.
8. Funny bits: fails with dust puffs and dizzy stars, sound effects, a side-eye to camera.
9. Keep: hook in 1-3 s, re-hook every 15-20 s, 61-70 s for TikTok pay, captions burned in.

## Main story format: "YOU invent it" (user idea, 2026-11-09)
Talk to the viewer as the inventor, breaking the fourth wall: "So you want X. Easy, you do Y... but there's a problem. So you get smarter and add Z..."
- Every part appears only as the fix to a problem the viewer just watched happen. No part is named before it's needed.
- Each "but there's a problem" is a re-hook (aim for one every 10-20 s).
- First try is dumb and funny (set fuel on fire -> the car is on fire). Fails get gags.
- Last line: "Congrats, you just invented the [real thing]" + one real-world wow.
- The viewer can be a character on screen (a little inventor or mascot) that the narrator talks to.
