# Touchscreen: "Dave builds a touchscreen" (video 4)

Format: vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, ~75 s. 2D hand-drawn world (kit/char2d boiling ink + Stick.sketchCtx for sets),
old-film look for the 1965 history beat (kit/fx/oldfilm.js), year badges on every era jump.
Plays in VideoHub's live preview (no render): `film/` follows VideoHub's project contract (renderOut(t), READY, VH_SCENES).
Every event is hung on a WORD (kit/timeline/words.js), so the real voice can replace the guide voice without retiming by hand.

Character: **Dave**, eager inventor, orange shirt + cap, gets every step a bit wrong. (Steve stays the deadpan of video 3.)
Story shape (FUN-RULES): want (a screen that feels his finger and pokes back) -> fails with gags -> the science -> payoff
that loops to the start (the haptic buzz finally pokes him back).
