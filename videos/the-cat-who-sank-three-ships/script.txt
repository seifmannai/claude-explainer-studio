One cat survived three shipwrecks. On both sides of the war.
Legend says in May nineteen forty-one, the German battleship Bismarck sank.
Hours later, British sailors found a cat floating on a plank.
They named him Oscar. Five months later, their ship was torpedoed. It sank. Oscar floated.
Now the sailors called him Unsinkable Sam.
So they moved him to the Ark Royal, the carrier that helped sink Bismarck.
Weeks later, a torpedo hit. She sank too.
And there was Sam, on a board, soaking wet and furious.
You see, Sam was never the unlucky one.
The navy sent him to live on land.
Lucky cat. Unlucky ships.
