## Facts to keep true (from the topic research)
- Ovens use 2.45 GHz, the same band as 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; ovens can disturb Wi-Fi (to verify with a source before the script is final).
- Heat comes from water molecules turning back and forth with the field (not a water "resonance").
- Waves reach only a few cm in, so thick or frozen food heats unevenly; ice absorbs poorly.
- Standing waves make hot and cold spots; the turntable evens them out.
- Metal box and door mesh: holes much smaller than the ~12 cm wave.
